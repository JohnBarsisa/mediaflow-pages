◉HOW ACCESS WORKS

Cloud or Personal Mode.

Cloud Mode uses Render for public links. For authorised media needing your account, Personal Mode runs on your Windows PC and can be opened privately on your iPhone through Tailscale.

Your account cookies are never sent to MediaFlow's Render server. No privacy, DRM or permission bypasses.

Use only media you own or have permission to save. Availability depends on each platform; no access bypass, DRM circumvention or guaranteed retrieval.

MEDIAFLOW · PRIVATE ACCESS

Use your own PC.

Some public posts return no media data on cloud servers. Personal Mode runs on your Windows PC using your own authorised browser session. Your login cookies stay on that PC.

  1. Install Tailscale on your Windows PC and iPhone, and sign into your private tailnet on both.
  2. On your PC, update MediaFlow, run start_personal_backend.bat, then run tailscale serve --bg --https=443 8000 in a terminal.
  3. With Tailscale connected on your iPhone, open your private PC address below. Never enable public Tailscale Funnel.

Full Windows 11 + iPhone setup guide ↗ Supported private *.ts.net addresses only. No cookies or passwords are sent to Render. Access and permission restrictions still apply.
